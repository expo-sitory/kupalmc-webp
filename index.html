Combat System

Every hit is a tiny math problem. HP is counted in half-hearts, so 2 HP = 1 heart.

Preface
  • Attack timer check: too soon after your last hit? Nothing happens.
  • Your power is worked out from class, items, level and runes.
  • Enemy defense shrinks the damage.
  • Roll for a crit.
  • Blocking can soften it, then the enemy loses HP and you may heal from lifesteal.
Note: "is 1 AD equal to 1 Damage?"

Nope, 7 AD = 1 damage. The game divides your AD by 7 before using it, then the target's armor shrinks it more (damage ÷ (1 + armor/100)). 1 damage = half a heart.

Example: 70 AD = 10 damage vs 0 armor, 5 vs 100 armor, ~3.3 vs 200 armor.

3 Kinds of Damage

  • Physical: from AD, shrunk by armor
  • Magic: from AP and rune powers, shrunk by MR
  • True: skips all defenses
  • Adaptive: (many runes) turns magic if your items give more AP than AD, otherwise physical. It also grows with league level (×1.07 at the start, up to ×1.90 at level 18).

Armor and Magic Resist

Defense divides damage:
damage taken = damage ÷ (1 + resist ÷ 100)
0 resist = all of it, 100 = half, 200 = one third, 300 = one quarter.

Pen goes first: flat pen is subtracted, then % pen cuts what's left.

Combat Mechanics

  • Crits: A crit does 1.75× damage and ignores blocking. After a non-crit you get a small bonus (up to +2.5% chance) that fades as crits chain.
  • Attack Speed: wait in ticks = 20 ÷ AS (20 ticks = 1 second). Waits with no items: Marksman ~1.2s, Assassin ~1.3s, Fighter ~1.7s, Mage ~1.8s, Support ~5s, Tank ~6.7s. Bows use the same timer.
  • Healing: Max HP = vanilla + class + item HP + shard HP + 4 per league level. Regen ticks every 5 seconds. Food doesn't heal you. Grievous Wounds cuts all healing, lifesteal and regen to 60%.
  • PvP: both players must have PvP on, and you can't hurt anyone more than 3 league levels away from you.

Skills

  • Mitigate: (shield or raised sword) cuts non-crit hits: wood 10%, stone 20%, iron/gold 28%, diamond/netherite 40%, shield 50%.
  • Parry: right-click a sword just before a hit lands. The hit does nothing, you get Strength II for ~2.5s, and the attacker is knocked back, slowed and weakened for 1.5s. Cooldown: 2s on success, 7s on a miss.
  • Dash: jump in mid-air. 7s cooldown, no fall damage for 3s after.
Rules of Thumb
  • Armored mob? Use magic or true damage.
  • Tough target? Get penetration.
  • Slow class? Make every hit count.
  • Parry beats a big hit, but a miss costs you 7 seconds.

Welcome to Season 1!

Embark on a journey across a meticulously crafted 1:40 scale replica of the Philippine archipelago!

Core Concept

Build, trade, and thrive as you establish your civilization on one of three major islands:

  • Luzon
  • Visayas
  • Mindanao

Twist: You choose your island, but your ethnicity is assigned randomly upon join. Adapt or perish.

Season 1 Features

[ Ethnicity System ]

Your ethnicity isn't just flavor! it's mechanical.

  • Unique Crafting Recipes – Each ethnicity unlocks exclusive recipes for nether and end items, forcing specialization and trade
  • Cultural Traits – Unique buffs and debuffs shape your gameplay:
    • Some ethnicities excel at mining and agriculture
    • Others master trade, magic, or warfare
[ Survival Mechanics ]
  • Nether & End Disabled – Traditional exploration banned
  • Ethnicity-Locked Progression – You must trade with other players to access forbidden resources
  • Mandatory Interaction – Solo play won't work; collaboration is survival
[ Economy & Trading ]

There is no NPC marketplace. No admin shops. Only player-driven economy

  • Establish trade routes between islands
  • Negotiate prices and treaties
  • Control rare resources through your ethnicity's monopoly
  • Build wealth through commerce, not grinding
[ Play Style ]
  • For players who want strategy and roleplay
  • For communities that love political intrigue and negotiation
  • For those tired of vanilla Minecraft
  • For builders with a purpose, create to expand your civilization
