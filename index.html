Combat System

Every hit is recalculated from the attacker's stats and the target's resistances, then applied as one custom damage instance. Vanilla melee damage and fall damage are discarded, and attack frequency is driven by attack speed. HP is counted in half-hearts, so 2 HP = 1 heart.

Hit Pipeline
  • PvP checks: both players opted in, level gap, no self-hits.
  • Attack cooldown: too soon after your last hit? Nothing happens.
  • Base damage: your stats against the target's resistances.
  • Critical strike roll: players only.
  • Life steal: players only.
  • Block mitigation: if the target is blocking and the hit did not crit.
  • Damage applied, then item and rune effects trigger.
Damage Balancer: 7.0

Total AD and AP are divided by 7 before they are used as damage. A displayed 70 AD deals 10 damage before resistances.

Stats

Sources: class base, vanilla attributes, items, rune shards, temporary modifiers, potions and enchantments. Only the first 6 league items in your inventory count.

Defensive
  • Health [HP]: A player dies when their health is reduced to zero. Bonus health is added on top of the vanilla 20, plus 4 per league level.
  • Health regeneration [HP5]: The amount of health a player passively restores per 5 seconds.
  • Armor [AR]: Reduces (mitigates) the amount of physical damage taken, thus increasing your effective health against physical damage.
  • Magic resistance [MR]: Reduces (mitigates) the amount of magic damage taken, thus increasing your effective health against magic damage.
  • Tenacity: Reduces the duration of slows by a percentage. No other debuff is affected.
Offensive
  • Attack speed [AS]: The number of basic attacks you are allowed to perform per second.
  • Attack damage [AD]: One of the two main offensive statistics, along with ability power. Deals physical damage on basic attacks.
  • Ability power [AP]: One of the two main offensive statistics, along with attack damage. Deals magic damage.
  • Critical strike chance [Crit%]: Denotes the chance that a basic attack will critically strike.
  • Critical strike damage: Increases the damage dealt when a basic attack critically strikes.
  • Armor penetration: When applying physical damage to an enemy, ignores a part of their armor in the damage calculations. Can be either flat or percentage-based. The target's armor is unchanged.
  • Magic penetration: When applying magic damage to an enemy, ignores a part of their magic resistance in the damage calculations. Can be either flat or percentage-based. The target's magic resistance is unchanged.
  • Life steal: How much health you restore, as a percentage of the damage dealt by your basic attacks.
Utility
  • Cooldown haste [CH]: Reduces the proc cooldown of runes / items.
  • Movement speed [MS]: How quickly you move across the map.
  • Saturation regen: The amount of hunger bars a player passively restores per 5 seconds.

3 Kinds of Damage

  • Physical: is innately mitigated by armor, a statistic that units possess.
  • Magic: is innately mitigated by magic resistance, a statistic that units possess.
  • True: cannot be mitigated by any innate statistic.

Armor and Magic Resist

Basic attack damage:
damage = AD × 100 ÷ (100 + effective AR)
Damage reduction is resist ÷ (100 + resist), so 100 resist is 50% and 200 resist is 66.7%.

Penetration lowers the resistance used in the calculation, not the target's actual resistance:
effective resist = max(0, resist − flat pen) × (1 − % pen ÷ 100)

Adaptive Damage

Adaptive damage is magic if your item AP is higher than your item AD, otherwise physical. It scales with league level:

Combat Mechanics

  • Critical strike: multiplier is 1.75 × (1 + CSD ÷ 100). After a miss you get up to +2.5% crit chance, shrinking by 0.5% per consecutive crit. Crits ignore blocking.
  • Life steal: damage × LS% × heal power. Grievous Wounds cuts life steal, heal power and regen.
  • Attack speed: cooldown in ticks = 20 ÷ AS (20 ticks = 1 second).
  • Blocking: shield 50%, wooden sword 10%, stone 20%, iron or gold 28%, diamond or netherite 40%.
  • Parry: right-click a sword just before a hit lands. The hit is cancelled, the attacker is knocked back, slowed and weakened, and you gain Strength.
  • Monsters: physical damage only, with no crits, penetration or life steal.
  • Fall damage: blocks fallen − 4 for falls of 4 or more blocks, plus a slow for every 10 blocks.
Example

A Fighter with an iron sword and 51 item AD hits a Zombie with 150 AR.

  • Total AD = 12 + 6 + 1 + 51 = 70, so AD = 70 ÷ 7 = 10
  • Damage = 10 × 100 ÷ (100 + 150) = 4.0
  • With 30 flat and 20% armor pen, effective AR is 96, so damage is 5.10
  • On a crit: 5.10 × 1.75 = 8.93

Welcome to Season 1!

Embark on a journey across a meticulously crafted 1:40 scale replica of the Philippine archipelago!

Core Concept

Build, trade, and thrive as you establish your civilization on one of three major islands:

  • Luzon
  • Visayas
  • Mindanao

Twist: You choose your island, but your ethnicity is assigned randomly upon join. Adapt or perish.

Season 1 Features

[ Ethnicity System ]

Your ethnicity isn't just flavor! it's mechanical.

  • Unique Crafting Recipes – Each ethnicity unlocks exclusive recipes for nether and end items, forcing specialization and trade
  • Cultural Traits – Unique buffs and debuffs shape your gameplay:
    • Some ethnicities excel at mining and agriculture
    • Others master trade, magic, or warfare
[ Survival Mechanics ]
  • Nether & End Disabled – Traditional exploration banned
  • Ethnicity-Locked Progression – You must trade with other players to access forbidden resources
  • Mandatory Interaction – Solo play won't work; collaboration is survival
[ Economy & Trading ]

There is no NPC marketplace. No admin shops. Only player-driven economy

  • Establish trade routes between islands
  • Negotiate prices and treaties
  • Control rare resources through your ethnicity's monopoly
  • Build wealth through commerce, not grinding
[ Play Style ]
  • For players who want strategy and roleplay
  • For communities that love political intrigue and negotiation
  • For those tired of vanilla Minecraft
  • For builders with a purpose, create to expand your civilization
